Run summary

Start or pick a run to see its summary.

Sending records a summary of every result in this run with today's date. You can keep recording and send again.

Pass means every step's "You should see" happened and nothing under "Look for" or "On every check" went wrong. Fail means anything did not happen as written, or looked or read wrong: say what you did, what you saw and what you expected. Blocked means you could not start or finish for a reason outside the check. Not provable means the test site cannot produce the event; it is never counted as a pass.

Results are saved as you go, on every device that opens this page. Nothing is deleted; every earlier version of a result is kept.
