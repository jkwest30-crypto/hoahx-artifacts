More pieces of work, all on the test site

Every one passed the website checks, and the final walk through every screen found nothing new and nothing worse.

  • On the test site

    The spreadsheet import, rebuilt. The import now has the app’s own look, a step bar, a preview of the first rows and long lists shown 50 at a time; email for a large import goes out at a pace the email service accepts.

  • On the test site

    Financial Ledger design begins. The first piece of the “Waiting for you” design and the designer’s next slice of screens are in.

  • On the test site

    Import and setup fixes. A treasurer who saw “Import a Spreadsheet” but could not start an import can now start it, and an officer who joins a second community keeps the setup prompt.

  • On the test site

    Tenants and balances. A renewed tenant is no longer turned away, a tenant’s hidden balance stays hidden, and the balance lookup now checks the right permission.

  • On the test site

    Backups and payment errors. Nightly backups retry when a first try is refused, with the restore steps written down; a payment error on a pay-now sale is recorded as unknown rather than guessed at.

  • On the test site

    Joining a home and the old AutoPay. Joining a home, the sign-in transition and the join review were fixed, and the old AutoPay checkout was closed off.
