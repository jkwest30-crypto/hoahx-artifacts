Start here

Six steps, in this order

  1. Read the rules.The next section is the set that does not move without a conversation. Everything after it is detail.
  2. Import the tokens before drawing anything.tokens.json has three sets: global, light, dark. Tokens Studio imports it directly; Figma Variables can be mapped from it. Build styles from it, not from screenshots.
  3. Name components the way the code does.Button / variant=destructive / size=sm, Badge / variant=warning, StatusBadge / status=overdue. The developer maps your file to src/components/ui by name.
  4. Design both themes at the test sizes.390×844 for phones, 1440×900 for desktop. Those are the viewports the automated tests use, so what you draw is what gets checked.
  5. If a design or code-generation tool helps, give it the block near the bottom.Paste the prompt block first and attach tokens.json. Ask for Tailwind 3.4 classes and existing @/components/ui imports, never raw hex or new packages.
  6. Deliver in the screen order and by its dates.A design that arrives before its build days goes straight in. One that arrives after them is a change to built work and is scheduled separately.

Out of scope for the launch

  • Migrating to Tailwind 4 or the current shadcn defaults.
  • Any new UI library, icon set, or typeface.
  • Redesigning the internals of the payment fields (NMI's embedded component, see Payments).
  • Passkeys as a sign-in method (after launch).
  • Phone layouts for management-company and platform-staff screens. They stay desktop.
  • A Storybook or component catalog site.

Words that stay stable

1,230 automated browser tests find elements by role and visible text. Colors, spacing, radius, icons, and layout can change freely. Renaming a button, heading, tab, or navigation item breaks tests until they are updated.

  • Navigation labels and sections come from the route registry. Reuse them.
  • Primary actions keep their verbs: Pay Now, Submit Request, Save, Cancel, Confirm, Delete, Approve, Deny, Invite.
  • Proposing a rename is fine: deliver old → new with the screen, and it is applied together with the test change.

Already decided

  • Both themes are live. Light / Dark / System toggle in the header and My Account. The default is being settled by the owners; design both.
  • Phones are a shell around the web app. The iOS and Android apps are this React app inside a native shell. Phone designs are the app designs.
  • Who gets phone designs: homeowners, tenants, and board members. Management companies and HOAhx staff use desktop.
  • Homeowners never pay HOAhx. They pay their community. The community's own subscription purchase lives on the web and is hidden inside the native apps.
  • Brand spelling is pending. The code says "HOA HX"; the owners' documents say "HOAhx". Use HOAhx and do not draw a new logo until they confirm.
  • Modules can be off per community (parking, amenities, dog park, voting, surveys, signatures, architectural review, visitor access, community feed, mass communications, analytics). Every module screen has an off state: it is simply absent.

Payments

The flow around NMI's embedded fields

Card, bank account, Apple Pay, and Google Pay fields are rendered inline by NMI's Payment Component: no redirect, no popup. It accepts a theme (Inter, primary, radius 10, light and dark) and nothing else about its internals can change. HOAhx draws everything around it. Seven states, phone and desktop, both themes:

Balance and scheduleAmount due, due date, grace period and late fee from the published dues schedule, Pay Now. Not yet set up: "Online payments are not yet available for this community. Contact your community administrator for payment instructions."
AmountFull balance or another amount, with a summary line.
MethodSaved methods (card brand and last four with expiry; "Bank •••3815") or new card / bank. Apple Pay and Google Pay buttons appear only where the device supports them.
FieldsThe embedded component. HOAhx shows the Pay button, the amount, and errors beneath it.
ResultPayment received with a confirmation number and an updated balance; or a decline with a plain message and a retry path; or Bank payment submitted, pending until the bank settles, then Paid. Returned bank payments reverse with a notice and a return-fee line.
AutoPaySave this method; AutoPay on or off; schedule on the community's due date including special assessments and late fees; next charge date; pause and resume; failed-charge alert; card-expiry notice three months ahead.
Receipts and historyEach payment with status, method, and a receipt.
Security

Sign-in flows, from your September 8 requirements

  • Setup, one step at a time: choose a method → verify it → save backup codes. Methods: authenticator app; text-message codes for residents who opt in. Roles that move money and HOAhx staff must enroll; residents are optional. Whether required roles may also use text codes is being discussed on Sep 14.
  • Code entry: InputOTP, accepts autofill and paste. "Can't access your device?" clearly visible on the challenge screen.
  • Recovery: backup codes, and a staff-assisted process that verifies identity.
  • Changing methods: requires verification; the new method is confirmed before the old one is removed.
  • Password: 15-character minimum everywhere, no composition rules, spaces allowed; stated plainly on register, invite, and reset.
  • App lock (native, Sep 23): after 15 minutes inactive or in the background the app locks and reopens with Face ID, fingerprint, or the device PIN. The biometric unlocks a session signed in with the code; it is not itself the second factor.
  • Account deletion: a resident can request deletion of their account; the request and its outcome are shown plainly.
  • Refunds: two people, each after a fresh sign-in prompt, original payment method only.

Copy

Words on the screen

  • Plain, resident-first, transparent. Every state says what happened and what happens next.
  • Money with cents ($250.00). Dates absolute (Oct 1, 2026); relative time only as a secondary line.
  • Status words match the StatusBadge presets: paid, pending, overdue, open, in progress, resolved…

Hand-off

Figma structure, naming, cadence

File structure

  1. Foundations: variables and styles from tokens.json (light and dark modes), type styles, effect styles.
  2. Components: one component per code name, variants named as the code names them, both modes.
  3. Patterns: page frame, sidebar, header, bottom tab bar, More drawer, homeowner header and nav, MobileSheet form, desktop table and its phone row, the empty / loading / error trio.
  4. Screens: one page per group in Screens, phone at 390×844 and desktop at 1440×900 where the screen exists on desktop, light and dark.
  5. Assets: icon, splash, manifest icons, screenshots.

Per screen, annotate

  • The route and the layout family.
  • Module flags that hide parts of it.
  • Every StatusBadge preset used.
  • Any label that differs from the registry, as old → new.
  • Tailwind classes where the intent is not obvious; spacing and color as token names, not hex.

If you commit code

How work gets into the repository

  • Node 22. npm install, then npm run dev. Real data needs the Firebase environment values from Jacob; the alternative is the emulator with a seeded demo community, which needs no credentials.
  • Branch from dev, pull request to dev. main requires a pull request and a green Build & test check. Every pull request runs the build, unit and rules tests, lint (an unused import is an error), a strict typecheck, and the 1,230 browser tests in five parallel shards: about 40 minutes. Keep pull requests to one screen or one primitive.
  • Tokens change only in src/index.css and tailwind.config.js. Components change only in src/components/ui. Nothing hard-coded in pages.
  • Do not run the shadcn CLI until Jacob has set "tsx": true in components.json; today it would write JavaScript files into a TypeScript repo. Then diff Button and Badge before accepting regenerated versions; both are customized.
  • Toasts: import from sonner only.
  • Tell Jacob before the first commit from a new GitHub account; the hosting account adds a paid seat for new committers and the owners decide that.
  • No branch previews are published automatically. Ask for one when you need it.

# HOAhx design constraints — paste this at the top of any design or code-generation prompt

You are helping design screens and components for HOAhx, a community-management web app for homeowners, HOA boards, and property managers, wrapped as iOS and Android apps. Design for THIS codebase, not for a generic product. When a rule below conflicts with your default, the rule wins. The full reference is `docs/design/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`; exact token values are in `docs/design/tokens.json`.

**Stack (fixed).** React 18, Tailwind CSS 3.4 (not v4), shadcn/ui "new-york" style on Radix primitives with class-variance-authority, lucide-react icons, the Inter typeface. Dark mode is class-based and every screen must exist in light and dark. Tokens are CSS variables holding HSL triplets (for example `--primary: 200 100% 34%`) used through Tailwind classes such as `bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`, `border-border`, `bg-card`. No new UI libraries, no new fonts, no Tailwind v4 syntax, no OKLCH.

**Color.** Use only these tokens: background, foreground, card, popover, primary (#0074AD in both themes), secondary, muted, accent (#3C83F6), destructive, border, input, ring, success (#29A847), warning (#F59F0A), danger (#ED2C2C), info (#3C83F6), demo (#9932CD), impersonation (#F97415), chart-1 to chart-5, sidebar-*. Light: ground #F8FAFC, card #FFFFFF, text #0F1729, border #DAE0E7, muted text #65758B. Dark: ground #121417, card #181A21, text #FFFFFF, border #282C34, muted text #646A78. Status tints for badges and banners come from the emerald / amber / red / blue / slate / orange / violet families (light: -50 background, -800 text, -200 border; dark: -500 at 15% background, -300 text, -500 at 25% border). Never introduce a new hue or a hard-coded hex.

**Type.** Inter only. Body 14px / 22px line height. h1 28px / 600 / tight tracking, h2 20px / 600, h3 17px / 600, card title 15px / 600, card description 13px muted, table header 12px uppercase with wide tracking, badge 11px / 600, bottom-tab label 10px / 600. Editable fields are 16px on touch devices.

**Shape and depth.** Radius 10px on buttons, inputs, and tab lists; 14px on cards and banners; 18px on menus; full pills on badges, switches, and avatars. Shadows are soft and low (the `card-shadow` pair). No gradients, no heavy drop shadows, no glass effects beyond the existing translucent headers.

**Components.** Use only the existing primitives in `src/components/ui`: Button (variants default / destructive / outline / secondary / ghost / link; sizes 36px default, 28px sm, 40px lg, 36px icon), Badge (default / secondary / destructive / success / warning / outline), Card, Dialog, AlertDialog, Sheet, Drawer, Tabs, Table, Select, Input, Textarea, Checkbox, Switch, RadioGroup, Popover, DropdownMenu, ContextMenu, Tooltip, HoverCard, Avatar, Progress, Skeleton, Separator, Accordion, Collapsible, Command, Calendar, InputOTP, Pagination, Breadcrumb, ScrollArea, Slider, Toggle, ToggleGroup, Carousel, Chart, and Sonner toasts; plus the in-house StatusBadge, RoleBadge, EmptyState, MobileEmptyState, MobileSheet, MobileSkeleton, AlertBanner (info / success / warning / error), ConfirmModal, and LoadingSkeleton. Name Figma components and variant properties exactly as the code does (`Button / variant=destructive / size=sm`). Toasts are Sonner, one system only.

**Layout.** Below 1024px: full-width content, a 56px top header, a bottom tab bar (four tabs plus "More") with safe-area padding, 44px minimum touch targets, bottom sheets instead of centered dialogs for forms and pickers, no hover-only interactions. At 1024px and up: a 240px sidebar (60px collapsed), a 56px header, and content in a page frame of 672 / 960 / 1200px or full width, with 16 to 32px padding. Design phones at 390×844 and desktop at 1440×900.

**Navigation and labels.** Navigation items, sections, and page titles come from a route registry and are asserted by automated tests. Reuse the existing labels (Dashboard, My Requests, Messages, Payments, Payment History, Assessments, Violations, Documents, Events, HOA Directory, Neighbors, My Household, My Account, and the admin equivalents: Member Management, Properties, Requests, Violations, Financials, Budget, Dues Schedule, Announcements, HOA Settings). If you propose a rename, list old → new explicitly and keep the old name in the design until it is agreed.

**Behaviour to design for.** Modules can be switched off per community (parking, clubhouse, dog park, voting, surveys, signatures, architectural review, visitor access, community feed, mass communications, analytics), so every module screen has an "off" state. A person can hold several roles in several communities and switches context from the header without signing out. System banners stack above the header: email verification, demo mode (violet), support impersonation (orange), and a community past-due banner for administrators. Every list has empty, loading (skeleton), and error states.

**Payments.** Card, bank account, Apple Pay, and Google Pay fields are rendered by NMI's embedded Payment Component. They can be themed (font, colors, radius, light and dark) but not redesigned. Design everything around them: amount, summary, Pay button, errors, pending "Bank payment submitted" state, confirmation with a confirmation number, receipts and history, saved methods shown as masked numbers, autopay status and next charge date.

**Accessibility.** WCAG AA contrast in both themes, a visible 2px focus ring, a real label on every control, roles and names that read correctly to a screen reader, and layouts that hold with reduced motion.

**Voice.** Plain, resident-first, transparent. Every state says what happened and what happens next. No jargon, no exclamation marks, no placeholder text such as lorem ipsum.
