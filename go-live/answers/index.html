HOAhx · Decision Register · the open questions

Open questions, answered by recommendation

Agree records the recommendation as your answer on every question the card covers. Change opens a box for what should be different; a sentence is enough. Nothing is final until you press Send, and you can come back to any card before then.

When you are through

Sending records your answers with today's date. You can change one and send again.

What happens next. An answer you agree with is recorded on each of its questions in the Decision Register and the build follows it. A change with your words is recorded the same way when it is clear, and becomes a short conversation when it is not. Every card names the questions it answers, by their register numbers, so you can find any of them on the register page.

Saved as you go, on every device that opens this page. Nothing here is deleted; every earlier answer is kept.
