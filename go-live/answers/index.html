Where you are

Screens

Each screen is answered on the screen itself. Open one, step through it, and press Signed off, Change or Discuss there. This page shows where each one stands.

Questions

Every question has our recommendation under it. Select a recommendation to read it in full, then Agree, Change or Discuss. When every question on a card is agreed, the card is agreed.

When you are through

What happens next on a question. An answer you agree with is recorded and the build follows it. A change in your words is recorded the same way when it is clear, and becomes a short conversation when it is not. A Discuss goes on the agenda for the next call.

What happens next on a screen. A screen you sign off is built as shown. A change becomes a follow-up item on the plan. A Discuss goes on the agenda for the next call.

Saved as you go, on every device that opens this page. Every earlier answer is kept.
